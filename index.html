Module 32020–202545 sec

Plot twist: marketing was enablement

The best content I made wasn't always for customers. A lot of it was for the teams selling and recruiting.

Codeup

BD intern → Digital Marketing
  • B2B: built focused sales-support content and landing pages for the team signing hiring partners. Employer partner leads went up 20%.
  • B2C: turned happy students into our best salespeople. Built a referral landing page, talking points for students, and an alumni Slack channel with direct access to staff for referral questions and ongoing support. Referrals were part of a +25% lift in student applications, a shared team result.
  • Interviewed hiring engineers about what they built, then translated it for career changers.
  • Planned, promoted, and sometimes hosted monthly events. Supported students in Slack.

Overland Partners

Senior Marketing Lead
  • Mentored two junior team members on business development, including RFP and proposal responses.

San Antonio Zoo

Manager, PR & Social
  • Ran content for a 6M+ follower audience, including education programs marketed to parents.
Key takeaway

Arm the people closest to the customer, sales reps or happy students, and they do the selling.
